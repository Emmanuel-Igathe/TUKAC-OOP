Help & Documentation
System guide, user roles, and frequently asked questions

Getting Started

Registration

New members register using their TUK Student ID and a valid university email address. You must upload a passport-size photo and indicate any registered disability (NCPWD number required if applicable).

After submitting, your account enters a Pending state. The Chairperson must approve it before you can log in.

Login & Password Reset

Log in with your email address or Student ID together with your password. If you forget your password:

  • Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.
  • Enter your registered email and Student ID to verify your identity.
  • Your password is reset immediately — you will be shown a temporary password.
  • Log in with the temporary password and change it from your Profile page.

User Roles & Permissions

The TUKAC Portal uses a five-tier role system. Each role inherits the permissions of the one below it. Your current role is shown in the top-right corner of every page.

👑 Chairperson Admin

Full system access. Responsible for overall governance of the portal.

  • Approve or reject new member registrations
  • Assign and change member roles
  • View, filter, and export the full security audit log
  • Delete members and manage all content
  • Generate all PDF reports (members, finances, audit)
  • Clear activity logs when required

🤝 Vice-Chairperson Executive

Assists the Chairperson in running the club. Manages events and member activity.

  • Create, edit, and delete events
  • View the full member list
  • Generate the members and finances PDF report
  • Post and moderate blog articles

📋 Secretary Executive

Keeps records and manages club communications and content.

  • Create, edit, and delete blog posts
  • Moderate forum discussions and comments
  • View member contact details and profiles
  • Generate the members PDF report

💰 Treasurer Executive

Manages all financial records of the club.

  • Record income and expense transactions
  • Delete or correct erroneous transactions
  • Generate the financial PDF statement report
  • View the current net balance and transaction history

👤 Member Standard

Regular club member. Base level of access after account approval.

  • View upcoming and past events; RSVP to events
  • Read blog posts and comment on them
  • Participate in forum discussions
  • View the current financial balance (read-only)
  • Update own profile, password, and disability details

Role Assignment

Roles are assigned by the Chairperson from the Manage Users section of the Admin panel. If you believe your role is incorrect, contact the Chairperson or club Secretary.

Key Features

📄 PDF Reports

Executives and the Chairperson can export professional PDF reports directly from the Admin panel:

  • Member List — All registered members with roles and status.
  • Financial Statement — All transactions with net balance summary.
  • Audit Log — Complete system activity history (Chairperson only).

Each PDF is branded with the TUKAC logo, report date, and the name of the officer who generated it.

🔒 Security & Audit Trail

Every significant action in the system is automatically recorded in the audit log:

  • User login and registration events
  • Password reset requests
  • Financial transactions added
  • PDF reports generated
  • Role changes and member approvals

Logs are viewable and filterable by the Chairperson under Admin → Activity Logs.

♿ Disability Support

The portal is designed to be inclusive. When registering, members with disabilities may provide their NCPWD number and disability type. This information helps the club arrange appropriate accommodations for events and activities.

💬 Community Tools

Stay connected through the Blog (curated announcements and articles) and the Forum (open discussion). All logged-in members can comment on blog posts and create forum threads.

Frequently Asked Questions

My account says "Pending" — what do I do?
Your registration is awaiting approval from the Chairperson. Contact the TUKAC Chairperson or Secretary and ask them to review your application in the Admin panel.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the login page, click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address and Student ID. A temporary password will be shown immediately — log in with it and update your password from the Profile page.
Why can't I see the Admin panel?
The Admin panel is only visible to the Chairperson. Other executives (Vice-Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer) have access to specific admin features (reports, finance, events) but not the full panel.
How do I download a financial report?
Navigate to the Finances page and click "Export PDF" (visible to Treasurer and above). The report will download automatically as a PDF file.
Can I update my disability information after registration?
Yes. Go to My Profile, update your disability type or NCPWD number, and click Save. Changes take effect immediately.
Who do I contact for technical issues?
Contact the TUKAC Secretary or Chairperson. For system-level issues, they can reach the portal administrator who has access to server logs and the activity audit trail.
