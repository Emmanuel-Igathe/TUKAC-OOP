Help & Documentation
Learn how to navigate and use the TUKAC Portal

Getting Started

Registration & Login

New members must register using their TUK Student ID and a valid email address. After registration, your account must be approved by the Chairperson before you can access the portal features.

If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset it using your Email and Student ID.

Key Features

📅 Event Management

View upcoming club events and RSVP to confirm your attendance. Executives can create, edit, and delete events. Reports on event attendance can be generated from the admin panel.

💰 Financial Tracking

The finances section displays the club's income and expenses. Members can view the current balance, while the Treasurer and Chairperson can record new transactions and export financial reports.

✍️ Blog & Forum

Stay updated with club news via the Blog. Engage with other members in the Forum by starting discussions or commenting on existing posts.

👤 Profile & Disability Info

Keep your profile updated, especially your contact information and NCPWD details. This helps the club provide better support tailored to your needs.

For Executives & Admins

Reporting

You can generate professional PDF reports for the membership list and financial statements. Look for the "Export PDF" buttons in the Manage Users and Finances sections.

System Audit Trail

Every critical action (logins, financial changes, role updates) is logged in the system for security and transparency. These logs are available to the Chairperson for auditing purposes.
