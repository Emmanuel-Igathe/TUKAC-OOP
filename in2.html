Who We Are

A Community Built on
Inclusion & Strength

The TUK Ability Club (TUKAC) was founded to create a safe, empowering space for students with physical, sensory, cognitive, and psychosocial disabilities at the Technical University of Kenya.

🤝
Peer Support Network Connect with fellow students who understand your journey and share resources across disabilities.
📣
Advocacy & Rights We work with university management to improve accessibility, inclusive policies, and NCPWD compliance.
🎓
Academic Empowerment Study groups, mentorship links, and academic accommodation guidance for all registered members.
🏅
Events & Competitions Adaptive sports, cultural events, leadership programs, and skills-building workshops open to all.

Our Mission Pillars

Three Pillars of Everything We Do

Every initiative, event, and decision at TUKAC is guided by these core commitments to our members and campus.

🛡️

Advocacy

We actively push for accessible infrastructure, disability-inclusive policies, and fair treatment for all students across the Technical University of Kenya.

🌟

Empowerment

Through skill-building workshops, mentorship, and leadership programs, we help every member unlock their full academic and personal potential.

🤲

Community

Belonging matters. We foster genuine friendships, peer support systems, and a culture where no student at TUK ever feels alone or unsupported.

Getting Started

Join in 4 Simple Steps

  1. 1

    Create Your Account

    Register with your TUK Student ID and university email. Upload a passport photo and, if applicable, your NCPWD number for disability verification.

  2. 2

    Await Approval

    The Chairperson reviews and approves new registrations, verifying that you are an active TUK student before granting portal access.

  3. 3

    Log In & Explore

    Once approved, log in to access your Dashboard — events, finances, blog, forum, disability profile, and your full membership record.

  4. 4

    Get Involved

    RSVP to events, start forum discussions, contribute blog posts, and grow your network within the TUKAC community and beyond.
